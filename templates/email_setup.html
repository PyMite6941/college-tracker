Email filters & setup

Mail is matched to a college by the email domains on each college profile, then sorted into a category with the keyword rules below.

1. Gmail filters (no password needed)

Gmail labels your college mail itself, as College/<college> and College/<category>. Filters only add labels and “never send to spam”. Nothing is deleted, archived or forwarded.

Import in Gmail: Settings → See all settings → Filters and Blocked Addresses → Import filters. Tick “Apply new filters to existing email” to sort old mail too. Download again after adding colleges or editing rules.

Category rules

One keyword or phrase per line. Matching is case-insensitive on whole words, and subject matches count three times as much as body matches. Mail that matches nothing is sorted under “Other”. You can still change a message's category by hand, and that choice sticks.
